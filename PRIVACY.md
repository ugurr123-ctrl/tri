# Tavriqelo Text Privacy Policy

Effective November 27, 2026

Tavriqelo Text expands user-defined shortcuts in supported editable web fields. When an expansion key is pressed, the extension checks the text immediately before the cursor to match a saved shortcut and inserts the corresponding snippet. The extension is designed to skip recognized password, verification code, and payment fields.

Snippets, folders, settings, custom variables, and usage statistics are stored locally in Chrome. This version of the extension does not send these records or field text to the developer, and does not use remote analytics or advertising. No account is required. If you export your snippets, Chrome saves the exported data to a file you control.

The extension runs on supported sites. It requests optional website access when you choose to enable expansion on additional sites. You can disable expansion, exclude websites, or revoke optional site access in Chrome.

You can delete snippets and settings through the extension where available, or remove the extension to clear its local extension storage. Exported copies remain under your control.

For privacy questions, contact the publisher using the support contact on the Chrome Web Store listing or open a GitHub issue without including private information.
